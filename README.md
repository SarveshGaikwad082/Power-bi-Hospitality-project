# 🏨 AtliQ Hospitality Revenue Optimization Dashboard (Power BI)

## 🎯 Objective
Analyze and visualize hotel **revenue performance** across cities, room categories, and booking platforms using Power BI to identify **price optimization opportunities** and improve overall profitability.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Overall Performance
- **Total Revenue:** ₹1.69 B  
- **Average Occupancy:** 57.8%  
- **ADR (Average Daily Rate):** ₹12.7 K  
- **Realisation Rate:** 70%  
- **RevPAR (Revenue per Available Room):** ₹7.34 K  

### 2. Revenue Contribution
- **Luxury Category:** 61.6% of total revenue  
- **Business Category:** 38.4% of total revenue  
- **Weekday Revenue:** ₹1.16 B (69%)  
- **Weekend Revenue:** ₹524 M (31%)

### 3. City-Wise Performance
| City | Revenue (M) | Occupancy % | Avg. Rating | Key Insight |
|------|--------------|--------------|--------------|--------------|
| **Mumbai** | 661 | 57.8% | 3.7 | Highest total revenue, but moderate occupancy – suggests scope to optimize pricing. |
| **Bangalore** | 415 | 55.7% | 3.4 | Low occupancy; requires pricing or marketing realignment. |
| **Hyderabad** | 321 | 58.0% | 3.7 | Balanced performance with stable realization rate. |
| **Delhi** | 291 | 60.4% | 3.8 | Strong occupancy; potential to increase ADR slightly for higher yield. |

### 4. Property-Wise Revenue Leaders
- **AtliQ Exotica:** ₹320 M  
- **AtliQ Palace:** ₹304 M  
- **AtliQ City:** ₹286 M  
- **AtliQ Blu:** ₹261 M  
- **AtliQ Bay:** ₹260 M  

### 5. Room Class Distribution
- **Elite:** ₹560 M (33%)  
- **Premium:** ₹462 M (27%)  
- **Presidential:** ₹377 M (22%)  
- **Standard:** ₹310 M (18%)  
> Premium and Elite segments drive ~60% of revenue. Standard rooms underperform — price or bundle optimization can improve yield.

### 6. Platform-Wise Analysis
| Platform | Bookings | Realisation % | Observation |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|--------------|
| **Others** | 54K | 70% | Highest share, consistent performance. |
| **MakeYourTrip** | 27K | 70% | Strong performer, potential for volume-based discounts. |
| **Direct (Online/Offline)** | 20K | 70% | Low share; improving direct channels can increase net revenue. |

### 7. Week-on-Week Trend
- RevPAR ↑ 0.2%  
- ADR ↑ 0.19%  
- Occupancy ↑ 0.01%  
- Realisation stable at 70.1%  
> Indicates **stable pricing** with slight revenue growth — suggests healthy demand elasticity.

---

## 💡 Recommendations for Price Optimization

1. **Dynamic Pricing Strategy:**  
   Implement weekday-weekend based pricing. Occupancy is higher on weekends (62.6%) — weekend rates can be raised by 5–8%.

2. **City-Specific Pricing:**
   - **Delhi:** Increase ADR by 3–5% due to high occupancy.
   - **Bangalore:** Offer discounts or loyalty promotions to improve occupancy below 56%.
   - **Mumbai:** Introduce rate segmentation — premium pricing for top-rated properties.

3. **Room Class Optimization:**  
   Standard rooms contribute only 18% of revenue. Introduce **value-add packages** (e.g., complimentary meals, upgrades) to boost realization.

4. **Platform Margin Control:**  
   Increase **direct bookings** by running exclusive website offers to reduce 3rd-party commission impact.

5. **Customer Experience Enhancement:**  
   Higher-rated properties (Delhi, Hyderabad) show better realization. Invest in service quality to improve ratings in low-rated cities.

---

## 🧠 Business Outcome
- Clear visibility into **revenue and occupancy patterns**.  
- Identified **price adjustment areas** by city and day type.  
- Data supports **targeted marketing and pricing strategy** for each room class and platform.
